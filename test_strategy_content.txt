Simple RSI Mean Reversion Strategy

This is a simple moving average crossover strategy for SPY.

Entry Rules:
- Buy SPY when the 9-day EMA crosses above the 21-day EMA
- Position size: 100% of available cash
- Enter at market price on the next trading day

Exit Rules:
- Sell when the 9-day EMA crosses below the 21-day EMA
- Exit at market price on the next trading day

Timeframe: Daily (check once per day at market close)

Risk Management:
- Stop loss: None (exit on signal only)
- Take profit: None (exit on signal only)
- Maximum position: 100% of portfolio

Testing:
- Test on SPY from January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2023
- Initial capital: $10,000
- Compare against SPY buy-and-hold

This is a simple trend-following strategy using exponential moving averages.
No complex options or multi-leg positions.
Should be straightforward to implement with basic Lumibot methods.
